# Draft caption: main cross-validation figure (R/fig_variance_explained.R, #36)

Numbers are from the final run (#35). Sources: `outputs/cv_level_skill.csv`, `outputs/cv_level_skill_by_insecticide.csv`, `outputs/cv_n_star.csv`, `outputs/cv_change_skill.csv`.

**Figure (`figures/CV_variance_explained.png`).**
Accuracy of bioassay-based estimates (green; paler for the nearest bioassay, further away) and of the models (blue: *mechanistic only*, the dynamical model; magenta: *full model*, the two-stage model) for the level of mortality in single held-out bioassays, in space (top) and ahead in time (bottom), with local data and without it.
Each bar is the % of variance explained, 100 (1 − MSE / Var(y)). (A) The interpolation test assays, predicted by the full-data fit, which included them: a prediction at a location with a bioassay. (B) The same assays, held out: no training data at their pixels, median 49 km from the nearest bioassay; 922 assays at 96 pixels. (C) Assays held out in the halves of five countries (Senegal, Côte d’Ivoire, Kenya, Ethiopia, DR Congo; two folds, one half of each country per fold, pooled), median 161 km from the nearest bioassay; 4,622 assays at 515 pixels. A and B score the same assays, so the step from A to B changes only whether the model saw the local data. (D) The assays of 2014–2018, predicted from the data before 2014: 8,327 assays at 1,348 pixels, median 50 km and 3 years from the nearest bioassay. (E) The same for 2018–2022 from the data before 2018: 3,805 assays at 718 pixels, median 32 km and 2 years.
Bars. *Local bioassay* (A): the expected score of a second, independent bioassay at the same pixel-year (100 − 2 × the noise share), computed from the bioassay overdispersion ρ̂. *Nearest bioassay*: the single nearest training bioassay of the same insecticide in the same or the previous year; in D and E, where no training data exist in the forecast years, in the last two years before the origin (2012–2013 and 2016–2017), so it forecasts by persistence. Among bioassays at the same distance (the same site), the most recent; among those, one at random. The distances and years in the panel titles are to this bioassay. The models' bars score the map: the posterior mean of population mortality (for the two-stage model, without its site-level noise terms u and p).
The key labels the parts of a bar: explained variance (coloured; the white line is the estimate and the lighter area its 95% interval), unexplained variance (dark grey) and bioassay noise (light grey), which no prediction of population mortality can explain. The dotted line between them is the score of a perfect prediction of population mortality, given the target's beta-binomial bioassay noise at ρ̂: 75.2% (A, B), 79.8% (C), 82.3% (D) and 86.3% (E).
n\*<sub>CV</sub>, the number of bioassays from the same pixel-year whose mean matches a bar's accuracy, is (100 − ceiling) / (ceiling − score): two-stage model 1.02 (B), 0.51 (C), 0.33 (D) and 0.38 (E); dynamical model 0.95, 0.46, 0.30 and 0.34; nearest bioassay 0.51, 0.31, 0.28 and 0.26; in sample (A), two-stage 2.59 and dynamical 1.12. Supplementary Table [S?] gives n\*<sub>CV</sub> per insecticide for B and C.
Intervals are 95% intervals from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of pixels; each resample also takes a posterior draw of ρ by insecticide, which enters the dotted lines and the local bar. Dotted lines are drawn at their point estimates: 67.9–80.3% (A, B), 77.4–81.8% (C), 80.6–83.7% (D), 84.8–87.7% (E).

**Figure (`figures/CV_in_sample_pooled.png`).**
In-sample accuracy of the models against 1–3 independent bioassays at the location, pooled, for level (A) and change (B). The bars and key are as in the main figure; the greens darken with the number of bioassays.
(A) The assays of panel A of the main figure, as % of variance explained. The bioassay bars are the expected score of the mean of 1, 2 or 3 independent bioassays at the held-out bioassay's pixel-year, each of its size: 100 (1 − (1 + 1/n) F / Var(y)), with F the data floor; 50.5%, 62.9% and 67.0%, against 53.1% (mechanistic only) and 65.7% (full model).
(B) Skill against no change: 100 (1 − Σ(P − A)² / ΣA²) (the persistence index; Kitanidis & Bras 1980). The target A is the change in mortality between a pair of single bioassays of one insecticide at one pixel, one in the five years before a forecast origin (2014 or 2018) and one in the five years after, at least 3 years apart, divided by the years between them; every prediction P of it is divided by the same gap. A pair entering at both origins is scored once. Each pixel-insecticide has equal weight. 0 is as accurate as assuming no change; below 0 is less accurate. 6,593 pairs (65% of all pairs; median gap 4 years) at 1,512 pixel-insecticides in 545 pixels. The bioassay bars are the expected score of the mean of 1, 2 or 3 independent pairs at the pixel, each with the target pair's noise over a gap drawn from the targets' gaps: 6.4%, 29.5% and 37.2%, against 10.6% (mechanistic only) and 21.7% (full model). The ceiling, 52.7% (46.2–57.8%), is low: a single pair of bioassays carries little information about change. A single pair scores little above 0 (−6.0–16.5%): it is right on average, but its large readings overstate the change.
Intervals as in the main figure.
